A family shop on Oleander

Tads Barber Shop is a locally owned barbershop at 4007 Oleander Drive, established by Tad Thomas. Public listings describe it as a family-oriented shop with several chairs on the floor.

The shop opens earlier than many others in town, which makes a weekday cut before work a little easier. Walk-ins are listed as welcome. Listings also note that a straight-razor neck shave is part of the finish of a cut.
