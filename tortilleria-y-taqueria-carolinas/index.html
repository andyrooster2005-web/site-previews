Tortillas first, then the rest of the table

Tortilleria Y Taqueria Carolinas is a family-owned tortilleria and taqueria at 419 College Road in University Landing Mall. Public descriptions say the corn tortillas are made fresh every morning.

That handmade start is the heart of the shop: tacos, quesabirria, tortas, and other plates built on tortillas that began the day in the same kitchen.
